Task : Sales Prediction

Dataset:
The dataset contains advertising spending for TV, radio, and newspaper, along with sales. It has 200 rows and no missing values. The extra row-number column was removed.

Models:
I trained and compared Linear Regression and Random Forest models.

Results:
Random Forest was selected for this run.
Random Forest: MAE = 0.66, RMSE = 0.79, R² = 0.98
Linear Regression: MAE = 1.46, RMSE = 1.78, R² = 0.90

Findings:
TV advertising had the strongest correlation with sales (0.78), followed by radio (0.58) and newspaper (0.23). These are relationships in this dataset; they do not prove that advertising alone caused the sales changes.
